Game UI concept mockup, 16:9 PC desktop game screenshot (mouse and keyboard, 1920x1080), NOT a mobile layout. The world-tree screen of a cute mushroom idle game: a big round world tree in the middle of a pale mint-green meadow, built from separate objects (bushes, rocks, stumps, small trees, flowers) placed around for depth, with small cute mushroom critters walking around. All characters, mushrooms and objects drawn with bold dark outlines and flat cel shading like the attached sample sheet. Interface: a slim top bar (screen title + tree level on the left, currency counters and a settings gear on the right), a compact left navigation rail with icon + label entries (active entry highlighted), a docked right-side info window with a title bar, section headers, compact rows and a progress bar, a hover tooltip near the cursor and a keyboard-shortcut hint strip at the bottom. UI COLORS: base panels in low-saturation pastel BEIGE / warm sand / oatmeal tones with darker beige-brown text and thin soft edges; BUTTONS are clearly more saturated and stand out (vivid leaf green primary, warm orange secondary, coral red danger) with a slight pressed lip. Small, dense but very readable text, consistent spacing, rounded corners. Korean labels, short.
