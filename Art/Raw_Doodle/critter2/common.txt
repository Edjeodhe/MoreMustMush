Character design sheet for a cozy mushroom-hunting game: tiny MUSHROOM CRITTER pets, redesigned with much more detail and personality in the style of the creature art of the mobile game "Doodle Pets: Survival" (뚜까펫 서바이벌, Farlight Games): chunky chibi creatures with a BOLD hand-drawn dark warm-brown outline, flat bright candy colors with one hard cel shadow and small highlights, big expressive faces, distinctive silhouettes, small fun accessories, readable at 64 px.
Each critter: a big mushroom cap as the head-top (unique shape, pattern and decoration per critter), a chubby round body with a face (big glossy eyes with white shine, small mouth, pink cheeks), two stubby little arms and two little feet, standing front view in a neutral idle pose with arms slightly away from the body and feet apart (it will be cut into parts for animation). Every critter has the same height and body size so they form one family, but each has its own body tint, eye style, expression and one small accessory that tells its power.
Sheet: a 2x3 grid on a fully transparent background, one critter per cell, centered with clear empty space around, nothing touching. No text, no labels, no ground, no shadows under them. Leave the last cell empty.
