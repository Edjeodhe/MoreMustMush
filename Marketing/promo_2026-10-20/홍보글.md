## 1. r/incremental_games

- Create Post → **Images & Video** → `pv_30s_1080p.mp4`
- 말머리(Flair): **Development**

**제목**
```
MoreMustMush – a cozy watercolor idle game where you bounce a harvester through a mushroom forest (free, browser)
```

**본문**
```
Hi! We're a small team from Korea and this is MoreMustMush, a free browser incremental game.

You pull back a little harvester, let it bounce through mushroom patches (a bit like breakout), and spend what you gather on the Mycelium Tree to grow faster and faster.

- 75 real mushroom species to collect in a Mushroom Book
- 80+ upgrades on the Mycelium Tree (more harvesters, bar skills, combos, gold)
- 5 regions with their own weather, and a boss every 10 stages
- A World Tree with mushroom spirits that keep gathering while you're away
- About 30 minutes per session, mouse only, saves in your browser, no login
- English, 한국어, 日本語, Русский, Українська

Play: https://minsung0421.itch.io/moremustmush

It's still in development, so feedback on pacing and balance is very welcome. Where did it feel slow? Where did it feel too fast?

**AI disclosure**
- Art: backgrounds, textures, character illustrations and the cover were generated with AI, then retouched by hand.
- Sound: sound effects are AI-generated.
- Code: partially written with AI assistance.
- Text/translation: the game is written in Korean; all translations (English, Japanese, Russian, Ukrainian) were done with AI.
- Made by hand: the whole game design (concept, mechanics, balancing) and art polish. We plan to replace more art with hand-drawn work over time.
```

**올린 뒤:** 첫 1~2시간 동안 댓글에 빨리 답하기. 밸런스 지적은 고맙다고 하고 메모해 두기.
