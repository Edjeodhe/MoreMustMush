The Little Ones

[[little_ones.png]]

Eight mushroom spirits join you as the World Tree grows. They start out as tiny sprouts and grow up into little forest folk. Give each one a spot around the tree and they keep gathering mushrooms while you're away.

[[deco_divider_clover.png]]

What's Inside

[[deco_worldtree.png]]

  • 75 real mushroom species to harvest and record in your Mushroom Book
  • 80+ upgrades on the Mycelium Tree: more harvesters, bar skills, combos and gold
  • 5 regions with their own weather, and a boss every 10 stages
  • A World Tree that grows through 8 stages, with a workshop and decorations
  • Skins for your harvesters and spirits

[[divider.png]]

Controls

  • Drag & release: pull back the harvester and let go
  • Move the mouse: slide the bar left and right
  • F / right-click: game speed x1, x2, x4
  • Esc: pause

Progress saves in your browser. English, 한국어, 日本語, Русский and Українська are in Options. Press the button at the bottom right of the game for fullscreen.
