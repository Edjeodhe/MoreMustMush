Welcome to the MoreMustMush 0.1.0 notes! This time we are not changing numbers, we are showing you around. Bouncing harvesters through mushrooms is only half the game: behind every round there is a World Tree to grow, a forest full of Little Ones, and a few very large mushrooms that do not want to be harvested.

— The MMM Team 🍄

PATCH HIGHLIGHTS

  • World Tree Decorations: 24 pieces to craft, place and enhance
  • World Tree Growth: evolve from Tier 1 to Tier 8
  • Bosses: 5 boss mushrooms, one every 10 stages
  • Idle Rewards: your Little Ones keep harvesting while you are away

WORLD TREE

🌳 Decorations

Context: A tree this big deserves lanterns, wind chimes and a crown or two. Every decoration also pulls its weight in your rounds, so dressing up the tree is never just for looks.

  • Collection: 24 decorations, 3 per Little One, crafted in the Workshop
  • Slots: 1 at Tier 1 ⇒ 12 at Tier 8 (up to +5 more from Little One levels)
  • Types: Hanging (trunk, branches, canopy) · Standing (around the roots)
  • Enhance: ★1 ⇒ ★5 using duplicates
  • Set Bonus: place a Little One's full set ⇒ that Little One's harvest +30%
  • First Placement: +5 Mycelium Stones

🌱 Growth

Context: Your harvest feeds the tree, and the tree feeds you back. Every tier makes room for more decorations and invites new Little Ones to move in.

  • Tiers: 1 ⇒ 8 (Tier 8: Arcana World Tree)
  • Tree Rings: Lv. 1 ⇒ 10 each tier, faster Tree Mushroom growth
  • Offering: stored mushrooms ⇒ World Tree EXP
  • Blessings: 8 round bonuses, all Blessing effects ×1.5 at Tier 8

BOSSES

Context: Every 10 stages a boss blocks the road. Five of them take turns, and each one asks you to play a little differently.

King Matsutake

PASSIVE: Has much more HP than other bosses. Bring your strongest harvesters.

Forest Cleaner

PASSIVE: Recovers 3% of max HP every 3 seconds. Keep the pressure on.

Angel of Death

PASSIVE: Removes all temporary harvesters on the field every 2 seconds.

Phantom Mushroom

PASSIVE: Summons clones at 66% and 33% HP. You must defeat the clones too.

Wandering Mushroom

PASSIVE: Keeps moving side to side, nonstop.

Boss Rules

  • Victory: bonus gold and mushrooms (×2) + Mycelium Stones (10 at Stage 10, +5 per boss after)
  • Defeat: 80% of the round's gold, you stay on the stage and the boss challenge pauses
  • Retry: turn the boss challenge back on from the Mycelium Tree whenever you are ready

SYSTEMS

🧺 Idle Rewards

Context: Little Ones love their tree. Send them to work on it and they keep picking Tree Mushrooms even when you close the tab.

  • Harvest Basket: stores up to 8 hours of picking (more with Little One and decoration bonuses)
  • Claim: gold + Mycelium Stones, and your Little Ones cheer you on
