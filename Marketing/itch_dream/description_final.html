How to Play

The Little Ones

Little mushroom spirits join you as the World Tree grows. They start out as tiny sprouts and grow up into little forest folk. Give each one a spot around the tree and they keep gathering mushrooms while you're away.

What's Inside

  75 real mushroom species to harvest and record in your Mushroom Book

  80+ upgrades on the Mycelium Tree: more harvesters, bar skills, combos and gold

  5 regions each with its own weather

  A boss every 10 stages with its own gimmick to break

  A World Tree that grows through 8 stages, with a workshop and decorations

  Skins for your harvesters and spirits

Controls

  Drag & release pull back the harvester and let go

  Move the mouse slide the bar left and right

  F / right-click game speed x1, x2, x4

  Esc pause

Progress saves in your browser. English, 한국어, 日本語, Русский and Українська are in Options. Press the button at the bottom right of the game for fullscreen.

Terms & privacy. Free to play. Please don't resell or redistribute the game files. We don't collect personal data and there's no login. The game sends anonymous play logs (a random ID and stage progress, no names or emails) so we can balance it.
