Features

  • 75 real mushroom species to harvest and fill your collection with
  • 80+ upgrades on the Mycelium Tree: more harvesters, bar skills, combos and gold
  • 5 regions with their own weather, and a boss waiting every 10 stages
  • A World Tree that grows with you, with 10 little critters who gather mushrooms while you are away
  • Skins for your harvesters and critters

Controls

  • Drag & release: pull back the harvester and let go to launch
  • Move mouse: slide the bar left and right
  • F / Right-click: game speed x1, x2, x4
  • Esc: pause

Your progress is saved in your browser automatically. Language can be changed in Options.
