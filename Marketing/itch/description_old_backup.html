You are a seasoned mushroom hunter in a world overrun by the Mushroom Apocalypse.
Harvest the mushrooms covering the land, record them, collect them all, and raise your very own quirky, adorable little mushroom buddies!

────────────────────────────

How to Play

  1. Launch your harvester in any direction you like.
  2. Bounce your harvester off the slider to power it up with buffs.
  3. Smash mushroom clusters to earn gold, and spend that gold to upgrade your skills.
  4. Fill your Mushroom Encyclopedia with your harvest and discover new mushrooms.
  5. Complete requests to help the World Tree grow.
  6. Tend to the World Tree together with the little mushroom friends who help you along the way!
