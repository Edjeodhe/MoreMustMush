MORE MUST MUSH — "Doodle cel" guide for SCREENS, BACKGROUNDS and UI (resource_test, 2026-10-15 4th pass). Reference for objects: the cute creature art of the mobile game "Doodle Pets: Survival" (뚜까펫 서바이벌).
GAME MOOD: a cozy, cheerful mushroom-hunting idle game. A kind old mushroom hunter (human grandpa) and his tiny mushroom critters harvest mushrooms with a pinball-like spinning harvester, grow a mycelium skill tree and a magical world tree. Warm, calm, toy-like, picture-book cozy; never dark, never busy.
OBJECTS (mushrooms, critters, grandpa, props, icons): bold, slightly hand-drawn dark warm-brown outline around the whole silhouette (sticker-like), flat bright candy fills, one hard cel shadow, tiny highlight. They must stand out clearly from the background.
BACKGROUNDS: FLAT, MUTED, PALE LIGHT-GREEN ground (soft sage / pale mint / light pistachio, low saturation, high lightness), almost a single flat color with only a few very subtle flat patches and tiny grass ticks; NO outlines on the ground, no texture noise, no clutter. Scenery objects (trees, bushes, rocks) are separate outlined objects placed only around the edges. The play area stays calm and empty so outlined objects pop.
UI: main color is a LOW-SATURATION PASTEL BEIGE family (oatmeal, cream, light sand, latte), with thin soft warm-brown edges and rounded corners, flat fills, very light inner highlight; no heavy wood frames, no thick dark borders, no glossy gel, no glow.
UI ACCENTS: buttons and important panels use a DIFFERENT hue with slightly HIGHER lightness and saturation than the beige so they clearly stand out, but still soft pastel-candy, not neon: primary = fresh sage-leaf green (#7cc46a), secondary = warm apricot orange (#f4a95a), info = soft sky blue (#78b8e8), danger = soft coral red (#ef7b6c), highlight panel = butter yellow (#f7dc7a) or light mint (#bfe8c8). Each accent button has a slightly darker same-hue outline and a darker same-hue bottom lip.
TEXT: dark warm brown (#4a3a2a) on beige, white with a soft dark outline on accent buttons. Korean labels allowed in mockups; rounded chunky font.
