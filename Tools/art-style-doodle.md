MORE MUST MUSH — "Doodle cel" visual guide for OBJECTS (mushrooms, characters, weapons, props). Reference: the cute creature art of the mobile game "Doodle Pets: Survival" (뚜까펫 서바이벌, Farlight Games).
- Cel-animation look: every object has a clear, bold, slightly hand-drawn dark outline (deep warm brown-black, about 4-5% of the object's width) around the whole silhouette, plus thinner inner lines for main details. The outline must separate the object cleanly from any background.
- Coloring: flat bright fills with exactly one hard-edged cel shadow tone (and at most one small hard highlight). No gradients, no painterly texture, no glow, no 3D rendering.
- Shapes: cute, chubby, rounded and simplified like doodle stickers; characters have big heads, tiny dot or bean eyes with a white shine, small mouths, pink cheeks. Mushrooms keep their real species' shape and colors but simplified and cute.
- Colors: bright saturated candy colors that pop strongly on a pale green background (coral red, orange, butter yellow, sky blue, violet, mint, warm cream, warm brown).
- Every object in a set shares one outline weight, shading style and level of detail; strong silhouette readable at 48 px.
- No text, no frames, no ground drop shadow unless asked.
