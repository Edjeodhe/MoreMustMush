MORE MUST MUSH — "Dream watercolor" guide (resource_test, 2026-10-15). STYLE IS THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE. When a style reference image is attached, copy its painting technique, palette and level of detail exactly.

MOOD: a quiet, dreamy, enchanted forest like the atmosphere of "Ori and the Blind Forest" — misty depth, soft backlight from far away, calm and a little magical. The mood comes ONLY from color, value and haze, never from effects.

TECHNIQUE: hand-painted watercolor on cold-press paper, but FLAT and MATTE.
- Every shape is a flat watercolor wash: one even color area with slightly uneven pigment, faint paper grain, and a soft darker pigment edge where the wash dried. At most ONE flat darker wash for the shadow side. No blending ramps.
- NO black or dark outlines, no ink linework, no strokes around shapes. Shapes are separated by value and color only (a slightly darker dried edge of the same hue is fine).
- MATTE: no gloss, no specular highlights, no shine, no rim light, no bloom, no glow halos, no lens flare, no light rays, no sparkles, no neon, no airbrush, no 3D rendering, no bevels.
- Simple big shapes, few details, no fine texture (no bark lines, no leaf veins, no grass blades, no fur). A child could cut each object out of paper.

PALETTE (limited, low saturation, harmonious; never candy, never neon):
- Forest: deep blue-teal, misty sage, moss green, dusty lavender, pale fog blue-grey.
- Light: warm cream, soft apricot, pale butter (used sparingly for faraway light and important items).
- Accents (small areas only): muted coral red, dusty rose, ochre gold.
- Shadows are cooler, bluer versions of the base color, never grey or black.

DEPTH (backgrounds): 3–4 flat layered silhouettes from back to front; far layers pale and hazy (fog, desaturated lavender-blue), near layers darker teal. Light comes from behind/far away as a soft pale area, not as rays.

MUSHROOMS: cute DEFORMED (chibi) versions of the REAL species: each must stay recognizable by its key traits (cap profile, real colors and markings, gills/ridges, ring/volva, clustering habit), but proportions are simplified and cute: big round cap, short chubby stem, few big markings, soft rounded silhouette. Never invented colors, never a generic toadstool. No faces on wild mushrooms.

CHARACTERS / OBJECTS: round, simple, chubby silhouettes readable at 64 px. Faces: two small dark dot eyes, tiny mouth, faint rose blush wash. They sit on the background as calm matte paper cut-outs with no outline; give them slightly warmer, lighter values than the background so they read.

FORBIDDEN (the "AI look"): glossy candy render, glowing particles, rim light, sparkles, dramatic god rays, hyper detail, busy texture everywhere, saturated rainbow colors, thick black outlines, sticker look, drop shadows, text, frames.
