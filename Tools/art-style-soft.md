MORE MUST MUSH — "Soft watercolor" visual guide (resource_test branch). Match the attached style reference images (round mushroom critter, sunny hill background, game logo).
- Rounded, chubby, soft shapes everywhere: blobby silhouettes, rounded tips and corners, gentle curves. Nothing sharp, spiky or realistic. Cute and simple.
- Flat watercolor coloring: each part is one flat color fill with a light watercolor wash feel (soft slightly uneven pigment, faint paper-like texture, a gentle lighter area where light hits). At most ONE soft shadow tone per part. No glossy highlights, no 3D rendering, no heavy gradients, no glow effects.
- Line: a thin, soft, slightly hand-drawn outline in a warm dark brown (like a colored pencil / brush pen line), not black and not thick. Inner details (spots, gills, eyes) are drawn simply.
- Colors: bright, clear and cheerful like a sunny day: sky blue, fresh leaf green, coral red, warm cream, butter yellow, candy pink, soft mint. Shadows are just a slightly deeper, warmer version of the base color.
- Faces (critters, characters): tiny dot or short oval eyes, a small curved smile, pink blush ovals. Simple and gentle.
- Backgrounds: flat anime-style scenery: clean flat color areas, puffy white clouds, simple bush and hill shapes, light soft shading, bright daylight.
- Readability: strong, simple silhouette that still reads at 64 px. Every asset in a set shares one consistent line weight, coloring and level of detail.
- No text, no labels, no frames, no ground drop shadow unless asked.
